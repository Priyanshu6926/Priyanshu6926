## 👨‍💻 Professional Summary

I am a **Final Year B.Tech Information Technology student** passionate about designing and building scalable software solutions that solve real-world problems.

My experience spans **full-stack web development**, **REST API design**, **real-time applications**, and **AI-powered systems**, with hands-on work using modern technologies including **React**, **Node.js**, **Express.js**, **MongoDB**, **Firebase**, and **Java**.
